Regional Transit Authority Approves Funding for New Rail Line

By Marcus Feld Updated Aug. 29, 2026

The Regional Transit Authority board voted 7-2 on Thursday to approve $340 million in funding for the long-delayed Riverside Line extension, clearing the way for construction to begin as early as next spring.

The extension would add six new stations along a 4.2-mile corridor connecting the downtown core to the airport district, a route transit advocates have pushed for since a failed 2014 ballot measure.

"This is the single largest infrastructure investment this board has approved in twenty years," said RTA chair Denise Okafor following the vote. "Riders have waited a long time for this."

Not everyone on the board was on board. Member Curtis Hale, who cast one of the two dissenting votes, argued the funding package underestimates long-term maintenance costs and pushed for a smaller, phased rollout instead.

Construction is expected to take four years, with the first trains projected to run in 2031. The RTA says fare revenue and a dedicated county sales tax increment will cover roughly 60% of the total cost, with the remainder coming from state and federal grants.

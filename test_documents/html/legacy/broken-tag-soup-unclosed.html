Uncle Ray's Weekly Fishing Report

Well folks another great week out on the lake, caught a whole mess of crappie

down near the old boat launch, water temp was about 68 degrees and clear as a bell. My nephew Todd came along and I gotta say

that boy is a natural, out-fished me three to one which I'll never live down at the bait shop.

Weather's supposed to turn Tuesday so if you're headed out get there early. Water levels are down a good foot from last month so watch for stumps near the north shore, lost a prop there once and it wasn't cheap to fix

  • Bait: nightcrawlers and small jigs
  • Best time: dawn, before the pontoon boats get going
  • Ramp fee went up to $5, bring exact change

That's all for this week, tight lines everybody and don't forget the club potluck is Thursday at the VFW hall, bring a dish if you can.

One more thing before I forget -- somebody left a green tackle box at the north ramp

Saturday evening, has a bunch of spinnerbaits and a pair of needle nose pliers in it. If it's yours give me a holler at the shop and I'll hang onto it behind the counter, no charge to claim it obviously it's your own gear.

Contact Also heard the marina down the road is finally reopening the fuel dock
after being closed since the flood back in June, about time if you ask me, been driving an extra fifteen minutes just to fill up the boat and that adds up over a season of weekend trips especially with gas prices the way they've been.
